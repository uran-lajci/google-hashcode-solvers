> **Saving the scarcer ingredient with small slices**
>
> Source: https://github.com/ROMBTeam/hashcode-practice-pizza
>
> Method overview:
> 1. Finds the "strategic" ingredient, the scarcer one.
> 2. At each free cell it lists the valid slices, keeps those with the least of that ingredient, and takes the smallest.
> 3. Then extends slices where it can.
>
> Our runs: sum of means 950,190 ([scores](../../output/strategic_ingredient_smallest_slice_greedy/scores.md)).
