> **Coverage greedy with random router removal**
>
> Source: https://github.com/AlexeyZhuravlev/hashcode-finals-2017
>
> Method overview:
> 1. `aleksey.cpp` places routers greedily by the cells they newly cover.
> 2. Runs 100 rounds of router removal with a random ratio between 0.5 and 1, and keeps the result when the score does not drop.
>
> Our runs: first on `opera` ([scores](../../output/coverage_greedy_with_random_router_removal/scores.md)).
